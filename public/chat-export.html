This is a hidden server-side timer triggered by the Admin/Digibord. - **Player State:** Players enter their name to join. Each player tracks their own score, collected items, and active cooldowns. - **Items:** - General Items (Sufficient supply, 1 point): Autosleutels, Huissleutels, Diploma, Ticket voor droomreis. - Limited Items (1x available globally, 2 points): Job Interview, Job raise (unlocks 1 min after Job Interview), Date. - **Real-Time Sync:** When a player claims a Limited Item, the server broadcasts an event. All other clients cross out this item on their map and show a toast: "[Naam] heeft de laatste [Item] gepakt!" - **Distractions (Ambient):** Fake push notifications slide down from the top every 30-90 seconds to distract the player.

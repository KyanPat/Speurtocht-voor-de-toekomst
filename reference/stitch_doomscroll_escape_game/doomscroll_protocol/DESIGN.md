## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a high-tension collision between addictive algorithmic social feeds and dystopian cyberpunk telemetry. It evokes psychological urgency, hyper-vigilance, and subversive dopamine cycles. The aesthetic confronts the user with real-time biometric and cognitive cost metrics, treating scrolling behavior as an existential survival game.

### Visual Style
- **Cyber-Dystopian HUD meets Algorithmic Feed:** Deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations punctuated by volatile neon surges (dopamine pink, toxic lime, threat amber, cyber blue).
- **Tactical Gamification:** Strict structural layouts layered with psychological pressure mechanics: live countdowns, glitch edge treatments, degraded posture states, and simulated high-priority push overrides.
- **Controlled Glitch & Micro-Vibrations:** Crisp, technical frames with razor-thin borders, corner reticles, scanline sub-surfaces, and chromatic aberration accents that intensify as psychological "Brainrot" thresholds escalate.

## Colors

The palette leverages pure obsidian and slate foundations as a deep, non-reflective canvas to maximize the optical shock of hyper-saturated neon indicators.

### Roles & Semantic Usage
- **Primary (`#FF1B6B` — Dopamine Pink):** High-urgency triggers, compulsive engagement alerts, dopamine surge buttons, critical cognitive drains, and unread loop counters.
- **Secondary (`#00FFA3` — Toxic Lime):** Active telemetry, "Vital" status levels, hyper-awareness bonuses, and validated survival objectives.
- **Tertiary (`#00F0FF` — Cyber Blue):** Neutral tactical data, system readouts, telemetry overlays, and secondary interactive state trackers.
- **Warning / Hazard (`#FFB800` — Cautionary Amber):** Escalate status notifications, FOMO streak warnings, and impending Brainrot transitions.
- **Neutral Canvas Hierarchy:**
  - Base Void: `#0A0C10` (Root viewport and infinite feed backdrop).
  - Deck Surface: `#131722` (Card backplates, floating HUD panels).
  - Elevated Plate: `#1B2130` (Nested metrics, input boxes, modal overlays).
  - Ghost Grid Lines: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.12)` (System dividers and bounding frames).

## Typography

Typography functions as both an urgent narrative voice and an operational terminal display.

- **Headings (Space Grotesk):** Highly structured, wide-stance, mechanical sans-serif designed for high-contrast impact and bold psychological commands. Always set tight with slight negative tracking on large titles.
- **Reading / Body (Hanken Grotesk):** Modern, sharp, and highly legible across rapid-scroll surfaces, balancing the severity of the surrounding HUD frames.
- **Telemetry, Timers, & Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced numeric tables, live brainrot degradation bars, verification codes, and time-remaining clocks. All uppercase labels require prominent letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror surveillance feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in a responsive 12-column grid on desktop and an optimized single-column infinite-feed rail on mobile viewports.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Strict 1-column stack pinned between a persistent top HUD header (tracking real-time Dopamine/Attention balance) and a fixed bottom tactical thumb-nav. Margins are locked to `1rem` to enforce edge-to-edge urgency.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Fixed-width central feed rail (`480px` to `640px`) flanked by persistent surveillance telemetry columns: left-hand psychological status charts and right-hand FOMO live event feeds.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a modular 4px/8px rule. Tight element gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) build dense information clusters, while `space-xl` separates distinct psychological trials and cycle checkpoints.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft natural lighting or neutral grey drop shadows. Instead, it operates through **emissive backlighting, tinted ambient fields, and structural line-work**.

- **Level 0 (Abyss):** Surface `#0A0C10`. Absolute canvas. May feature a subtle 1px cyan grid overlay at 4% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panel):** Surface `#131722` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. No blur.
- **Level 2 (Active Target):** Surface `#1B2130` with a 1px border of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` and an ambient inner glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (Alert / Dopamine Surge):** Bordered with solid `#FF1B6B` accompanied by a localized neon bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 27, 107, 0.35), 0 0 2px #FF1B6B`).
- **Scanline & Flicker Layer:** Tactical status overlays utilize horizontal repeating CSS scanline stripes (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` at 2px intervals) combined with hardware-accelerated micro-opacity shifts during high-stress interactions.

## Shapes

The design uses a sharp, industrial aesthetic. Curvature is kept low to maintain a clinical, software-terminal tone.

- **Base Radius:** `0.25rem` (`roundedness: 1`) applied to inputs, buttons, and alert tags.
- **Panels & Container Decks:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Tactical Cutouts:** Critical action buttons and HUD overlays use 45-degree chamfered clip-paths (`polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 8px) 0, 100% 8px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) on top-right corners to emphasize diagnostic hardware equipment.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to transient status badges (e.g., "LIVE AGONY", "ACTIVE STREAK") to mimic standard high-engagement algorithmic tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary ("Dopamine Surge"):** Background `#FF1B6B`, label JetBrains Mono Bold in `#0A0C10`. Features a 45-degree chamfered corner and intense hover bloom. Active state applies a 1px negative offset simulating a physical click switch.
- **Secondary ("Neutralize / Bail"):** Translucent obsidian base with a 1px border in `#00FFA3`. Text in toxic lime. On hover, shifts to solid `#00FFA3` with black text.
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, uppercase monospaced text with blinking cursor prefix (`> ESCAPE_LOOP`).

### Metric Cards & Feed Units
- **Feed Quest Card:** `#131722` base with a 1px top border in neon cyber blue. Includes corner bracket markings (`+` glyphs at coordinates), live countdown timers in the top-right corner, and variable background grain.
- **Badge Cards:** Micro-cards displaying active multipliers (e.g., `3.4x BRAINROT INDEX`) with saturated neon outline accents and high-contrast monospaced integers.

### Psychological Vitality Gauges (Vital vs. Brainrot)
- Segmented linear bar displays with 10 to 20 granular ticks.
- **Vital State:** High-frequency `#00FFA3` pulses with stable steady-state labels.
- **Brainrot Degradation:** Gradient shift from `#00F0FF` through `#FFB800` to flashing `#FF1B6B`, supplemented with subtle text jitter animations and strikethrough warnings.

### FOMO Indicators & Push Alerts
- **Fake System Intercepts:** Floating, high-z-index cards styled as urgent operating system warnings (`SYS_ALERT: ATTENTION FRACTION AT 12%`).
- **Crossed-Out Items:** Strikethrough lines rendered in bold `#FF1B6B` with a live "MISSED" stamp rotated at -6 degrees.
- **Pulsing Banners:** High-visibility banner docks using alternating diagonal hazard stripes (black and `#FFB800`) with high-frequency marquee text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Terminal Input Fields:** Dark `#0A0C10` fields recessed into `#1B2130` plates. 1px cyan borders that intensify upon focus, complemented by an active blinking neon block cursor.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp, square geometric selectors (`roundedness: 0`). Active state displays an interior neon dot with hard glowing borders.
